## Terms used in the ERPAccredited Service Provider (ASP)In the UAE, a provider approved to exchange e-invoices over the Peppol network and report them to the tax authority. The ERP submits the PINT AE invoice to your provider.Activity / WBSA line of the project work breakdown structure. Costs and budgets are tracked per activity.Analysis typeA setting on a ledger that asks for an extra breakdown (employee, vehicle, project, asset, supplier, customer or custom) whenever the ledger is posted.AP / ARAccounts Payable (what the company owes suppliers) and Accounts Receivable (what customers owe the company).API logThe Hub’s record of every call: what was sent, what came back and when. Its rows are chained with hashes so that a change afterwards is detected; secrets are hidden.B/L (bill of lading)The carrier’s receipt for the cargo. Its quantity is the quantity of a freight invoice.BOMBill of material: the list of components, with quantities and scrap allowance, needed to build one panel. It has revisions and can have options (see Option class).BOQBill of Quantities: the priced list of contract work, used for the contract value and cost budget of a project.BunkersThe fuel a vessel burns. Bunker bills are voyage costs of type Bunker.CIPCapital work in progress: cost collected on an asset or project that is not finished yet.CoA (contract of affreightment)A contract to carry a quantity of cargo over a period, in several voyages.ConcessionAcceptance of a unit that failed a test (“use as is”), approved by the role named in the approval rule.Control accountA ledger that summarises a sub-ledger, such as Accounts Payable for all suppliers. Suppliers and customers sit under their control ledgers.Cost centreA part of the business used to group costs (a department, a site, a vehicle pool).Counter chain (ICV / PIH)For ZATCA, every invoice carries a counter (ICV) and the hash of the previous invoice (PIH). The Hub sends the invoices one at a time, in order, so the chain has no gap.CSIDThe certificate that ZATCA issues to each e-invoicing unit. It is obtained in the onboarding steps and used by the gateway to sign invoices.Custom reportA report that you build yourself in Reports › Custom Reports, from any tables of the ERP, and save for later use. Only the layout is saved; each run reads the live data.Data dictionaryThe list of every table of the ERP, grouped by module, with its fields and the tables they link to. Reports › Custom Reports › Data Dictionary.Data source (report)The main table a custom report starts from. Each row of the report starts from one record of it.DemurrageMoney the charterer pays when loading or discharging takes longer than the allowed laytime: hours over × the demurrage rate.DespatchMoney the owner gives back to the charterer for time saved against the allowed laytime, usually at half the demurrage rate. Recorded as a credit note.Direct tax invoiceA sales or purchase tax invoice entered without orders or delivery notes. Every line goes straight to a ledger account that you choose, as a debit or credit, with a tax code. See Direct Sales Tax Invoice and Direct Purchase Tax Invoice.DrydockTaking a vessel out of the water for survey and repair at regular intervals.DWT (deadweight)The weight in tonnes a vessel can carry, including cargo, fuel and stores.E-Invoice RegisterThe list of every invoice and credit note prepared for ZATCA or the UAE provider, with counter, hash, status and the authority’s answer. It is never cleaned automatically.ECLExpected credit loss under IFRS 9: an allowance for receivables that may not be collected, based on their age.ECR / ECOEngineering change request and order: the approved way to change a released bill of material or routing.EOSBEnd of service benefit (gratuity) under the UAE Labour Law: 21 days of basic salary per year for the first five years, 30 days per year after.ExceptionA record of the attendance that is wrong or incomplete: a missing punch, shift or job code, a budget mismatch or an overtime alert. Every exception must be resolved before a month can be reconciled.ExtensionOne configured connection of the Extensions & API Hub (for example KSA ZATCA or a ZKTeco terminal), with its settings, mapping, trigger, retry rules and log.First-pass yieldThe share of units that pass an inspection the first time they are tested.FreightThe price of carrying cargo on a voyage charter, usually a rate per tonne of the B/L quantity.GatewayA small program on a server of yours that does what a browser cannot: TCP to devices, SFTP, SMTP, ZATCA signing, calls to services that do not allow web pages, and receiving webhooks.GDNGoods Delivery Note: proof that goods left the store for the customer.Grace periodMinutes after the shift start (or before the shift end) that are not counted as late (or early).GRNGoods Received Note: proof that goods or services were received from a supplier.GRN PayableA clearing liability for goods received but not invoiced yet. The purchase invoice clears it.Group By (custom report)Turns a custom report into a summary: the columns that are ticked Group form the groups, and the other columns are summed, counted or averaged for each group.ICVIn-Country Value: the UAE programme that scores suppliers on local spend, used in tenders.IMO numberThe seven-digit identity of a vessel; the last digit is a check digit.IPCInterim Payment Certificate: the periodic certificate of work done that the client approves before billing.Job IDThe payroll project code that hours are charged to. A timesheet line has a Job ID, a Phase and an Activity.JVJournal Voucher: a manual accounting entry.LaytimeThe time the charter party allows for loading or discharging, counted in hours.LC / LGLetter of Credit and Letter of Guarantee issued by a bank.Live modeThe master switch of the Hub. While it is off every extension is answered by the simulator; real messages need it on, the extension’s Simulation set to No, and the credentials.Locked VATThe VAT amount that the system calculates from the tax code and shows greyed with a lock. Change the amount or the tax code instead of typing the VAT.LPOLocal Purchase Order sent to a supplier.MOL / WPSMinistry of Human Resources (labour) and the Wage Protection System through which salaries are paid by bank SIF file.MRPMaterial requirements planning: proposes what to make and what to buy from demand, stock and open orders.NCRNon-conformance report: the record of a failure (a failed test, faulty material, a complaint) and the decision taken on it.NRVNet realisable value: stock is written down when it is worth less than its cost.Option classA choice that varies a panel (for example IP rating or controller). Lines of the bill of material can depend on the option chosen.Out of scope (VAT)A supply or cost outside the scope of VAT, for example wages, fines or dividends. The tax code has 0% and the amount is in no box of the VAT return.Payroll categoryA group of employees paid and controlled together (Direct Labor, Staff …). It decides who is reconciled, timesheeted, processed and paid together.PDCPost-dated cheque.Phantom itemA group of components shown as one line in a bill of material but not stocked; it is blown through into the parent order.PINT AEThe UAE e-invoice data model (UBL 2.1, Peppol International) that the ERP builds and submits to the Accredited Service Provider.PostingWriting a document's accounting entry into the General Ledger.Prompt at run timeA filter whose value is not saved but asked each time the report runs, usually a date range or a company. It keeps a saved report useful for ever.PunchOne IN or OUT record of an employee from an attendance device, a file or typed by hand.Reconcile & ApproveLocks the attendance of a month for a category, once every exception is resolved, so that the timesheets can be generated.RetentionPart of a contract value held back by the client (or by you from a subcontractor) until completion or maintenance ends.ReversalA new journal that cancels a posted one. The original stays visible.RoutingThe steps to build a panel, with the work centre, setup and run times, crew and quality checkpoints of each.ShiftThe working hours of a day: start, end, break and grace minutes. A shift that ends before it starts is a night shift.SimulationThe mode in which the Hub answers like the real service from a built-in simulator, including failures you can trigger. Nothing leaves the ERP.SOAStatement of Account for a supplier or customer.Time charterHiring a vessel for a period at a daily rate; the charterer decides where it goes.Time keeperThe person who loads the punches, resolves the exceptions and prepares the timesheets, with access to Time Management only.TravellerThe job card printed with a production order that follows the panel through the factory.TRNTax Registration Number issued by the Federal Tax Authority.Unbilled revenueWork done for a customer that has not been invoiced yet (a contract asset).UndertimeBudget hours less normal hours and leave: the hours an employee was expected but did not work or take as leave.UtilisationThe share of the days in service that a vessel was on a voyage.VaultThe encrypted store in the ERP for passwords, keys and tokens of the extensions (AES-256-GCM, key from your passphrase). The passphrase is never stored.Voyage charterHiring a vessel for one voyage between named ports at a freight rate.WebhookA message that an outside system sends to a web address when something happens (for example a payment). The gateway keeps it; the ERP collects it and checks the signature.WIP (Work in progress)The cost of orders that have started and are not yet received into finished goods.Work centreA place where work is done (a bench, a bay) with its capacity and its rates per hour.Yellow rowA row of the Payroll Run whose payable hours differ from the budget hours by more than the tolerance. It needs an adjustment reason before the payroll is approved.ZATCAThe Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority of Saudi Arabia, which requires e-invoices in the Fatoora format (Phase 2: integration with its platform).
