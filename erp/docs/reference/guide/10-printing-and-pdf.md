## Printing and PDF 

All documents print in one company format: company name and address, Arabic name, a title badge (TAX INVOICE, PURCHASE ORDER…), the number, date and party strip, an information box, the line items with VAT, the totals, the amount in words, a computer-generated note, three signature boxes and a generated-on footer. 

_[Screenshot: The print preview of a purchase order]_

The print preview of a purchase order 

In the print preview, choose A4 Portrait or A4 Landscape, choose which signature boxes to show, then select Print / Save as PDF. To make a PDF, pick Save as PDF as the printer in the browser's print window. Reports have their own Print, PDF and Excel buttons. The document view has ✉ Email and 📎 Attachments buttons beside Print.
