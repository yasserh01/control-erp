## The life of a document 

Transactions move through statuses. The buttons on each row change to match. 

Draft→ Submit →Submitted→ Approve / Post →Approved / Posted→ Convert →Partially …→Fully … / Closed  DraftSaved but not final. Can be edited or deleted. Documents that post directly (LPO, GRN, invoices, notes) go straight from Draft to Approve / Post; others need Submit first. Submitted / Under ReviewWaiting for approval. Someone whose role has the Approve / post permission sees Approve or Post. ApprovedAccepted, with no accounting yet (requisitions, orders, quotations). It can now be converted to the next document. PostedThe accounting entry is in the General Ledger. A posted document cannot be edited; it can only be reversed. Partially / Fully …Shows how much has moved to the next document: Partially Received, Fully Invoiced, Partially Paid, Paid. RejectedSent back. Edit it and submit again. CancelledVoided before posting. Kept for the record with the reason. ClosedFinished. No further conversions.  

Approval limitsIf an Approval Rule covers the document type and amount, only the named role can approve it. A role without Approve / post permission never sees those buttons. Postings into a month that is closed in Period Control are refused until the period is reopened. A month locked in Period Lock refuses every change, not only postings. 

### The Actions menu 

Actions on a document row opens every other option for that document. What appears depends on the document and its status.

Converting an LPO to a GRN: choose the quantity now for each line  View / Print PDFOpens the printable document in the company format (see below). EditOnly while the document is Draft or Rejected and not posted. CopyOpens the data entry window of a new voucher that holds the data of the original: header, lines, narration and analysis. The next number in the series is already filled in, the date is today and the status is Draft. Change what you need and save. If you close the window without saving, nothing is created and the number stays free. Posting results, approvals and payment references (cheque number and date) are not copied. In a locked month a copy dated today is refused. EmailOpens the Email window of the document: the recipient is taken from the supplier, customer or employee record, and the subject and text are ready. Under Files to send tick the document as a PDF and any of the files attached to it, then select Send. The ERP sends the email itself, shows the result, and records it in the Email Log. This needs the one-time set-up in Email Settings; until it is done, or if you prefer your own program, Open email app opens the message there and you attach the PDF yourself with Print / Save as PDF. A document in a locked month can still be emailed. Also available as ✉ Email in the document view. Accounting EntryShows the journal lines the document has posted, or will post (a preview) if it is not posted yet, with project, activity and cost centre. From here you can print the journal or open the General Ledger. Print Journal EntryPrints the accounting entry as a journal voucher. Document FlowShows the chain of related documents (PR → LPO → GRN → Invoice → Payment). Select a number to open it. Analysis DetailsShows or enters the analysis split (employee, vehicle, project, asset…) for lines posted to accounts that need analysis. Convert to …Creates the next document from this one. You choose the lines and the quantity for now; the rest stays open for later (partial delivery, partial invoicing). Return to Supplier / Return Goods / Create Credit NoteStarts a return or a credit note linked to this document. Generate Payment / Generate ReceiptOn a posted invoice with a balance due: creates the payment or receipt voucher already linked to the invoice. Void / ReverseOn a posted document: posts a reversing journal dated today and marks the document as reversed. The month must be open. Void / CancelOn an unposted document: asks for a reason and marks it Cancelled. Documents made from it must be voided first. DeleteRemoves a draft completely. Not available once a document has postings or later documents; the menu says why. AttachmentsAttach files (quotations, delivery notes, signed copies, emails) to the document: PDF, images, Excel, Word, text, and email files (.eml, .msg). Choose several files at once, up to 400 KB each. The list shows who added each file and when; View shows it and has a download link. The count shows in brackets. On vouchers, Save and attach saves the entry and opens this window. Nothing can be added or removed on a document dated in a locked month. Also available as 📎 Attachments in the document view. Audit TrailOpens the audit log filtered to this document number.
