## The home screen and the top bar 

This part describes the Classic view (big tiles). The ERP now opens in the new interface described in the next section; the pictures here show the Classic view, which you can bring back at any time with Classic view in the top bar. 

The home screen shows one large tile per module. Select a tile to open the module. Each module opens a hub with three cards: Masters & Settings (the set-up records the module uses), Transactions (daily entries and processing) and Reports. Selecting a card lists its screens in small groups.

The home screen also has a Find a screen box: type part of a name (supplier, payroll, VAT, stock…) to jump straight to any screen. The bar at the top of the ERP is always visible:  Title and pathThe name of the screen you are on and the module and group above it. ← ReturnGoes up one level: from a screen back to its group, from a group back to the module hub, from the hub to home. All modulesJumps straight back to the home screen. A− A+Makes all text smaller or larger. The size is remembered. Role / Sign outBefore sign-in is turned on, a list shows the role you are working as (Administrator by default). After sign-in is on, it shows Sign out (username). ThemeSwitches between the light and dark colour themes. Company selectorOnly in multi-company mode: choose the company you are working in, or All companies (Group view). Every entry, report and print follows this choice.
