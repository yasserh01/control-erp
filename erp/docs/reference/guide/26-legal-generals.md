## Legal & Generals

The company's own licences and certificates (trade licence, Civil Defence, immigration card, Ejari, chamber membership, VAT and corporate tax registration, ISO certificates, insurance policies), tracked to their expiry with alerts, documents and an activity log. 

Start with Certificate Types (they come pre-loaded with the usual UAE certificates and their validity and alert days), then add each certificate in Company Certificates with its dates and a scan of the document. The Compliance Status page and the Overview alert you before anything expires. A type marked as an operating licence (such as the trade licence) also warns, or blocks, new purchase orders and supplier payments while it is expired, according to Certificate Settings.

### Masters & Settings  

### Certificate Types

Legal & Generals › Masters & Settings › Certificate Types 

Defines how each kind of certificate behaves: category (statutory or operational), issuing authority, validity in months, alert days before expiry, whether a yearly surveillance audit is needed (ISO) and whether it is an operating licence. These values are the defaults for new certificates of the type. How to use it
- Select + New type (or Duplicate an existing one).
- Enter the name, category, issuing authority, validity in months (0 = does not expire) and alert days.
- Set Yearly surveillance audit and Operating licence, add guidance text, and Save.

### Certificate Settings

Legal & Generals › Masters & Settings › Certificate Settings 

The default alert days, which roles can add, renew and delete certificates, and what happens when an operating licence has expired and someone creates a purchase order or supplier payment (off, warn, or block). It also lists the UAE rules the register follows. How to use it
- Select Change settings, set the default alert days, tick the roles, choose the expired-licence behaviour and Save settings.

### Transactions  

### Company Certificates

Legal & Generals › Transactions › Company Certificates 

The register of company certificates. Each one is tracked to its expiry, holds its scanned document and raises an alert before it expires. Tabs filter by Expired, Expiring, Valid, No expiry, Inactive, Audit due and No document. How to use it
- Select + Add certificate and choose the Certificate type first: name, authority, alert days and expiry are suggested from it.
- Enter the certificate number, Issue date and Expiry date (or tick does not expire).
- Choose who is Responsible for the renewal and attach the document (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 400 KB).
- Save. When renewed, open the certificate and record the new dates and document.

### Reports  

### CEO & BOD Dashboard

Legal & Generals › Reports › Management Dashboards › CEO & BOD Dashboard (also in the Reports hub under Legal & Generals Reports) 

One screen for the Chief Executive and the Board of Directors: where the money is (bank position and the next 13 weeks), what customers still owe (expected receipts), what the company owes (payables) and how profit and the projects are doing. Every figure is read live from your records and is the same figure you see on the Bank Position, Receivable and Payable Ageing, Financial Statements, Budget Entry and Project Cost Control screens. The screen only reads; nothing you do on it changes a record, apart from the two text boxes for your own commentary. How to use it
- Select Reports › Legal & Generals Reports › CEO & BOD Dashboard, or Legal & Generals › Reports › Management Dashboards.
- Choose the period at the top right: This month, Last month, This quarter, Year to date or Last 12 months. The results (revenue, profit, EBITDA, budget) are for that period and are compared with the period before it. The position (bank, receivables, payables, the 13-week forecast, projects) is always as at today. The line under the title says so.
- Read the six cards at the top. Select a card to open the screen behind it. Hover over any bar, line or ring to see the exact amount.
- Move between the five tabs to see more detail. Every list row that is an invoice or a project opens that document or its report.
- Type your explanation in Management commentary (Overview) and Notes on operations (Projects and operations). Each box is saved when you click away and is kept separately for each period. The button Add the points for the Board copies the plain-language points the screen has worked out into the commentary for you to edit.
- Select Board report (A4) to open the four-page report, then Print or Save as PDF. Select Edit commentary in the report window to go back and change the text. Excel saves the KPI table as a spreadsheet. Refresh reads the figures again. 

The six cards
| Card | What it shows | Opens | 
| Bank and cash | Balance of all bank accounts plus petty cash, less any overdraft used, compared with the end of last month, with a six-month trend. | Bank Position | 
| Net profit | Net profit for the period, the margin, the previous period and the budget (pro-rated to today). | Financial Statements – profit or loss | 
| Receivables | All open customer invoices, split into not yet due and overdue. | Receivable Ageing | 
| Payables | All open supplier invoices, what falls due in the next 7 days and what is overdue. | Payable Ageing | 
| Working capital | Current assets less current liabilities and the current ratio, compared with the end of the previous period. | Financial Statements – financial position | 
| Cash in 13 weeks | The projected bank and cash balance at the end of week 13 and the lowest point on the way, from the Bank Position forecast. An estimate for payroll is deducted (see the notes below). | Bank Position – forecast by week |

Buttons on this screen
- Period changes the period for the results.
- ⟳ Refresh reads the figures again.
- Excel saves the KPI table as a spreadsheet.
- Board report (A4) opens the four-page report for printing or saving as a PDF.
- Add the points for the Board adds the worked-out points to the commentary. 

Good to know
- The dashboard adds up all companies together. To see one company, use the company selector at the top of the ERP first.
- Cash in 13 weeks comes from the Bank Position forecast: open invoices by due date, post-dated cheques, loan instalments and deposits at medium confidence or better. Because salaries are not an open document, the screen deducts an estimate based on the last three payroll runs and says so wherever it uses it. Check the figure against the real payroll before relying on it.
- The budget is the one entered in Budget Entry. The current month is pro-rated to today, so the comparison is fair part-way through the month. With no budget entered the screen says so instead of showing zeros.
- A project with no estimate to complete shows no expected margin rather than 100%. Enter the estimate in the project master or the Cost Control screens to see it.
- The commentary and the notes are kept per period (for example Year to date in October is separate from Year to date in November). Saving a box is recorded in the audit trail.
- If something cannot be calculated, the dashboard shows a message naming the screen to check instead of a blank page. It never changes data.
- The page and the report use the dark and light themes and fit a phone screen; the report prints on A4. 

Who can open it
- Roles that have the Legal & Generals module and the Sensitive data permission, because the dashboard shows company-wide profit, bank and payroll-linked figures. Other users see a message saying which permission is needed. The same applies to the tile in the Reports hub.
