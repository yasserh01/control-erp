## The new interface: menus, side menu and Quick actions 

The ERP opens in a calmer layout with a menu bar at the top, like the finance systems many accountants already know. Every screen, report, print and email works exactly as before; only the way you move between screens is new. Select Classic view in the top bar (or Use the classic home and menus at the foot of the home screen) to return to the big-tile home screen, and Try the new interface at the top of that screen to come back. The choice is remembered on this computer.  Top barThe system and company name (with the company selector in multi-company mode), the Find a screen box, and the buttons Guide, Email log, Alerts, A− A+, Theme, Classic view and the role list or Sign out. Press / on any screen to jump into the search box, type part of a name and press Enter. Alerts opens Overview and shows a dot when approvals or document expiries are waiting. Module menuThe row under the top bar has one button per module, in this order: Home; Overview, Finance, Legal, Reports; HR & Payroll, Time, Master Data, Settings; Projects, Purchase, Sales, Inventory, Manufacturing, Fleet, Assets. You see only the modules your role may use. The button of the module you are in is marked. Masters, Transactions, ReportsInside a module a second bar shows its menus. Select Masters, Transactions or Reports to see every screen of that menu in groups, then select a screen to open it. Esc closes the menu. A module whose screens are organised differently shows its own menu names. Side menuOn the left, a tree of the same screens for the module you are in, with the current screen marked. Select a group to open or close it. Collapse menu hides the tree to give the screen more room. On a phone the tree opens from the Menu button. Favourites and RecentOn any screen select Add to favourites to keep it one step away; select it again to remove it. Favourites and Recent are lists in the module bar and in the side menu, and the last screens you opened appear on the home screen. They are kept for your user name only. Home screenThe home screen shows the modules as cards in three groups: Finance and control (Overview, Finance, Legal & Generals, Reports, and Group & Intercompany in multi-company mode), People and administration (HR & Payroll, Time Management, Master Data, Users & Settings) and Operations (Projects, Purchase, Sales, Inventory, Manufacturing, Marine Fleet, Fixed Assets). Select a module name to open its hub with big tiles, or a link on the card to go straight to its Masters, Transactions or Reports. Quick actionsThe card on the left of the home screen starts the work you do most. The standard set is New journal voucher, New purchase order (LPO), New sales invoice, Customer receipt, Supplier payment, Run payroll, Party statement (SOA) and Stock receipt: the ones that create a document open its entry form at once. Select Customise, tick the screens you open most (up to 12), filter the list by typing, and select Save. Each user has his or her own Quick actions; Reset to standard brings back the standard set. Phone and tabletOn a phone a bar at the bottom has Home, Search, Favourites and Menu. The module row scrolls sideways. Light and darkTheme switches between the light and dark colours as before; the page background stays the same teal in both.  

Printing, PDF and email are not affected: the menus and bars never appear on paper.
