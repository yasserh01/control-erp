## Overview

The live dashboard: figures read from your posted records for the period you choose, compared with the same period a year earlier, with notifications and items waiting for approval. 

The Overview is the first module on the home screen. Nothing on it changes data; it only reads. Select any figure or chart to open the screen behind it. 

_[Screenshot: The Overview dashboard]_

The Overview dashboard 

### Parts of the dashboard 

| Part | What it shows |  
| Period and company filters | This month, Last month, This quarter, Year to date or Last 12 months; and the company (or all). ⟳ Refresh reloads, Print prints, + New opens a quick menu to create common documents. |  
| Module bar | One button per module for quick navigation. |  
| Headline figures | Revenue, Net profit, Cash and bank, Receivables, each with the change against the comparison period. |  
| Financial position | Payables, Inventory (at cost), Fixed assets (net) and VAT payable (output less input). |  
| Operations | Open purchase orders, Delivered not invoiced, Overdue invoices, Awaiting approval. |  
| People and projects | Active employees, Payroll cost, Leave and EOSB provisions, Open projects (and how many are over budget). |  
| Financial performance | Revenue and costs by month, Where the money goes (cost of sales and expenses), Net profit by month, Cash and bank by month, Receivables and payables by age. Each chart has a Table view. |  
| Needs attention | Notifications: expiring employee, vehicle and company documents, project closings, certificates waiting, stale items. Awaiting approval: documents waiting for your role. |  
| Projects and customers | Project status (progress and cost against budget) and Top customers by invoiced sales. |  
| Summary | Profit and loss summary for the period against the comparison, and Recent activity from the audit log. |  

 

TipStart each day on the Overview: clear the notifications and the approvals waiting for you, then open the modules from the module bar.
